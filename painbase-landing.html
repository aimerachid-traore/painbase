How PainBase works

listen → cluster → surface
01 / LISTEN

We never stop scraping

Workers continuously pull posts & comments from Reddit, Hacker News, Dev.to, Twitter/X and forums where people vent about unsolved problems.

02 / CLUSTER

AI groups the noise

Every post is classified, de-duplicated and clustered by embeddings, so hundreds of separate rants become one quantified problem.

03 / SURFACE

You get the brief

For each problem, the AI writes a summary, scores demand & competition (0–100), and suggests a product angle you could actually ship.

Common questions

before you start
Where does the data come from?

PainBase scrapes Reddit, Hacker News, Dev.to and Twitter/X continuously. Every post and comment is processed by an AI (Groq / Llama) to detect whether it expresses a genuine unsolved problem, then clustered with similar signals and stored in the database.

What is the Opportunity Score (0–100)?

The AI scores each problem on three dimensions: Demand (how many people express the pain), Competition (how saturated existing solutions are), and Opportunity (the gap between demand and solution quality). A score above 70 means high unmet demand with weak competition.

How often is the database updated?

The pipeline runs continuously and processes new posts every hour. Trend scores are recalculated weekly by comparing mention volume from the last 7 days against the previous 7-day period.

Is it really free?

Yes — PainBase is free during the beta. You can browse the leaderboard and preview problems without an account. Create a free account to unlock the full database, search, and your personal dashboard. No credit card required.

Can I save and track specific problems?

Yes. Once you create a free account, you can bookmark any problem from its detail page. Your saved problems appear in your dashboard so you can track trend changes week over week.
